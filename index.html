Interactive simulator accompanying Davis & Gómez-Ramírez (forthcoming), A model for teaching the macroeconomics of tariffs with global supply chains.

Instructions

First, set the scenario. Then choose the central bank’s interest rate period by period.

Scenario

0 < πT ≤ 30 · in %, omit the % sign

0.1 ≤ β ≤ 2

0 ≤ iMP−1 ≤ 100 · in %, omit the % sign

55 ≤ Y0 ≤ 145 · pre-tariff Y = 100

55 ≤ Ye,0 ≤ 100 · pre-tariff Ye = 100

Your decision

For each decision, assume that:

  • an increase (decrease) of 0.25 in it reduces (increases) Yt+1 by 1 unit;
  • an increase (decrease) of 0.25 in it increases (reduces) Ye,t+1 by 0.25 units.

Answers are checked to two decimals. If your answer has a repeating decimal, you can enter it rounded to two decimals or as a fraction (e.g., 15.33 or 46/3).
